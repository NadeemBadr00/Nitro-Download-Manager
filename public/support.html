Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I download videos from YouTube, Facebook, and LinkedIn?

With the Nitro DM Chrome Extension installed, open any video page. A floating "Download Video" button will appear automatically on the bottom-right. Click it to choose your desired quality (1080p Full HD, 720p, 480p, or MP3 Audio) and start downloading.

Where are downloaded files saved?

By default, files are saved directly in your Windows Downloads directory (e.g. C:\Users\<YourUsername>\Downloads). When a download completes, click "Show Folder" on the toast notification to open and highlight the exact file in Windows Explorer.

How does Turbo Multi-Threading work?

Nitro DM divides large files into up to 32 concurrent HTTP chunk streams and downloads them simultaneously. Once all parts are downloaded, the engine automatically merges them into the final file, maximizing your network connection speed.

Does Nitro DM collect any personal data?

No. Nitro DM runs 100% locally on your machine. We do not track your browsing history, downloads, or IP address. For full details, review our Privacy Policy.
